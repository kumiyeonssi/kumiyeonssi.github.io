Case Study

Sliceo

Website and marketing design direction for a consulting and advisory firm serving community association management and PropTech organizations.

  • RoleWeb design, UI direction, content layout
  • WorkWebsite sections, LinkedIn graphics, visual strategy
  • FocusClarity, operational storytelling, premium consulting feel
  • ToolsFigma, WordPress, Photoshop, AI-assisted creative direction

Overview

Sliceo needed a stronger digital presence that communicated advisory expertise without feeling like a generic software or consulting website. The design direction focused on clear messaging, modern editorial layouts, and visuals that make operational complexity easier to understand.

Design direction

  • Premium consulting style with clean spacing, confident typography, and calm layouts.
  • LinkedIn visuals that explain business value quickly, including before-and-after operational graphics.
  • Campaign assets designed to feel connected to the brand instead of one-off social posts.
